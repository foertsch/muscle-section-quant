What the readout counts

Collagen outside the fibers = red pixels in the main section that lie in no fiber outline, divided by all red + yellow pixels in the main section.

Blue is what is counted: endomysium, perimysium, epimysium, tendon, nerve and vessel walls. Green is red that falls inside an outline and is not counted, mostly where an outline edge overlaps the septum next to it.

Unstained areas (glass, holes from freezing artifacts, tears) are left out of both numbers. Colour classes follow the rule of Hildyard et al.

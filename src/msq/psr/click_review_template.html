How to score a window
  1. Judge only fibers whose middle lies inside the window.
  2. Missed fiber: a fiber with no outline. Click inside it.
  3. Merged: one outline around two or more fibers. Click inside that outline, once.
  4. Split: one fiber cut into two or more outlines. Click on the fiber, once.
  5. Not a fiber: an outline around collagen, a vessel, a hole or a fold. Click inside it.
  6. Leave correct outlines alone. Turn outlines off (O) to check a fiber's edge.
  7. Click Window done when a window is finished, even with no marks.
